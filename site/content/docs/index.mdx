*For two readers: the customer's engineer, who wrote the originals, and the executive and account audience who has to decide on the basis of them.*

AnyCompany runs agentic auto-loan fraud underwriting on Snowflake Intelligence — eight
specialist fraud agents plus a master synthesizer, all written by their own engineer. This site
records what happened when those nine agents were ported onto **Amazon Bedrock AgentCore**
without rewriting a single prompt.

## Four headline results

| Result | Figure | Artifact |
|---|---|---|
| End-to-end adjudication latency | **31.45 s p50 / 46.55 s p95** (n=42) | `evals/results/pipeline_14x3_us-east-1.json` |
| Runs under the customer's 60 s target | **40 of 42** | same file |
| Cost per adjudication | **$0.1440 p50** (n=41 priced runs) | same file |
| Specialists held on Claude, on one deterministic metric | `_context` grounding **0.642 Claude vs 0.357 GPT** | `evals/results/set_comparison.json` |
| Master synthesis, on **GPT-5.6 Luna** | **4.20 s p50** (n=42), down from 55.2 s on Claude | `evals/results/pipeline_14x3_us-east-1.json` |

Derivation, per-specialist breakdown and every caveat: [Measured results](/docs/measured-results).

This is a **mixed-model system**, and both halves of that were measured rather than assumed: eight
Claude specialists and one GPT-5.6 synthesizer. GPT-5.6 was also tested for all eight specialists
across **72 blind-judged analyses** and declined — a projected 47.8% cost reduction turned down on
one compliance measurement. [GPT-5.6 on this platform](/docs/gpt-5-6).

## One rule this site follows

Every number traces to an artifact committed in the repository, and the page quoting it names
the file. Where a figure was not measured, the page says **NOT MEASURED** instead of
estimating. Where two artifacts disagree, both appear and the authoritative one is named.

That rule is stated up front because an earlier draft summary for this engagement broke it.
It claimed *"130 analyses over 8 domains, judged by GPT-5.6 Sol, band 23/26 per model, 12.3 s
end-to-end at $0.0508"* — and none of those figures appear in any artifact on disk. Our real
cross-family study is [120 analyses over 4 domains with two
judges](/docs/model-selection/cross-family-study); the real measured end-to-end is
[31.45 s p50 at $0.1440 p50 over 42 runs](/docs/measured-results). Detecting that class of
error is why every percentile here carries its `n`.

## One question that blocks the cost case

AnyCompany reports a projected **$700K–$1M** annual Snowflake AI spend. Measured cost
here is **$0.1440 per adjudication** (p50, n=41). Comparing those two needs a figure the
customer has not supplied: **how many applications per day take the full eight-agent forensic
path.**

| Applications/day through full 8-agent underwriting | Annualised at measured p50 |
|---|---|
| ~3,000 | **~$158K** |
| ~300,000 | **~$15.8M** |

Arithmetic: volume x 365 x $0.14395847. The p50 is measured; the two volumes are illustrative
brackets, not the customer's figures. Same architecture, opposite conclusion — which makes this
the single most important open item: [Snowflake comparison](/docs/cost/snowflake-comparison).
